AI Meter 0.3.2 for Windows
==========================

Start: double-click "AI Meter.exe".
AI Meter opens in its own window. Close the window to stop it.

First time only: Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because this
app is not signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Your settings and data are kept in C:\Users\<you>\.aimeter
If something goes wrong, the details are in C:\Users\<you>\.aimeter\aimeter.log

Keep this whole folder together: AI Meter.exe needs the "python" folder next to it.
